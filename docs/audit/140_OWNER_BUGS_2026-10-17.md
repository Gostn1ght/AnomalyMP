## Fixed (13d637546, bc58db5a8)

- **Rotor turning with the engine off.** The Mi-2's `idle` motion covers
  every partition; `PlayCycle(MotionID)` then plays it once per partition
  and returns null, so the stored blend pointer was null and the speed was
  never written: the rotor always ran at full speed. The speed is now set on
  every blend of the model (`LL_IterateBlends`), every frame, from the rotor
  speed (clients: replicated rpm, zero with the engine off).
- **No fall damage for connected players.** Movement is the owner's, so only
  the owner's client sees the ground contact (`CActor::g_Physics`) and sends
  the landing as a `GE_HIT` strike on its own actor. The anti-cheat filter
  dropped every client `GE_HIT`, so remote players never took fall damage
  (from a helicopter or anywhere). Accepted now: only `GE_HIT`, destination
  = the sender's own actor, who = weapon = that actor, strike type, power
  0..2, finite impulse, payload length checked, one per 250 ms. It can only
  hurt the sender. Every other client hit stays refused.
- **Helicopter without a pilot.** `netcoop_engage` records the pilot. With
  nobody in the pilot's seat there is no collective: the helicopter falls
  (the engine also stops, as before). An impact that loses
  `heli_crash_speed` (12 m/s) faster than 30 m/s per second
  (the decaying "recent speed") kills it through `ChangeCondition`, so the
  normal death/explosion reaches every client. Gentle landings never trigger
  it (fixture: 6 m Ctrl descent).
- **Corpse loot "no space".** Rucksack cells were max(120, 4 per carry kg); a
  few GAMMA rifles (10-12 cells each) filled them while the weight was fine.
  Now 8 per kilogram (cap 512; the 512-object save limit is unchanged).
  A refused ground pickup now tells the player the reason (it was silent).
- **Client log flood.** Dot Marks' campfire prompt read `db.actor` every frame
  on a client without an Actor (bind_campfire.script:20); 20000-line log
  trims destroyed the evidence of the session. Guarded.

Fixtures: `check-native-helicopter.py` (pilotless fall and single impact
crash, gentle landing), `check-native-inventory-capacity.py` and
`check-hideout-transaction.cpp` (8 cells/kg), `check-netcoop-helicopter.py`
(new key), Lua vehicle prompts.

## Transitions: characters stuck after a crash (0ee644d76)

The released build on the m2btest stand (eca858c03, its master database kept
from the previous hard-stopped run): one bot passed 22 times, the other two
were refused at every login, "active, in transit or has an unfinished save on
another location". The master kept them ACTIVE (or FROZEN in a passage) on
Garbage while their local files named Cordon - what a location crash or a stop
during a passage leaves behind. Login routing reads the local file first, the
master only afterwards, so such a player could never play again.

- The master's slot inspection now answers `ELSEWHERE` with the home location
  name (nothing else) when the character lives outside any transfer on another
  map, ACTIVE or DISCONNECTED. Frozen / in-transit stays refused (expired
  unclaimed passages are already returned by e3fcbf1f1).
- The location server sends the player to that map (the usual redirect); if it
  is down or starting the player is told so (on-demand maps are woken).
- Older location servers treat the new status as a refusal, as before.

Tests: master suite 340 pass (the two tests that asserted a refusal across
locations now assert the home answer, without mutation); world-writer parse
cases; native admission scenario (active and disconnected elsewhere).
